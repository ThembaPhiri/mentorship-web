Our objective

To connect IT graduates already working in the industry with graduates who are still looking for their break, so that experience, skills and confidence are passed on inside our own community instead of staying locked in individual careers.

  • A clear path: certifications, portfolio, CV and interview prep
  • An insider view of different IT specialisations
  • Confidence and a professional network
As a mentor
  • Sharpen your leadership and communication skills
  • Give back to the community that shaped you
  • Build your reputation among peers
  • Grow a network of future colleagues
